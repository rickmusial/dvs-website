Why founders can't judge their own ideas — and how to get the honest read that saves them.

by Rick Musial

There are good tools now that will walk you through validating your idea for free. Real structure, real steps. And they still won't tell you the truth about your idea — for one reason that never goes away: you're the one holding the prompt.

You can't be your own second opinion. This book is about the read you can't give yourself — and how to go get it.
